# A calmer payment operation

Countersign's purpose is to reduce the repeated work of reconstructing agreements, confirming delivery, coordinating approvals and closing the books. The long-term product earns more delegated trust only when customers can see correct outcomes and retain independent control.

The first market hypothesis is small crypto teams paying recurring contractors from Safes. Read-only onboarding should be useful before any authority is granted. The next investment is real customer cycles, measured administrative effort, live Circle/Safe compatibility and independent security review, followed by carefully approved small allocations.

A software subscription and separately priced reserved liability are hypotheses to validate with operating cost and actual exposure. No revenue, customer count or loss-rate claim is established yet. Expansion follows demonstrated customer need; general ERP, yield strategies and marketplaces are separate products.

The day after a release still requires an incident owner, reconciliation, customer support and claims operations for every outstanding coverage window. Passing tests or entering an event does not end those responsibilities.
