# Every payment should have a current reason

Countersign's purpose is to reduce the repeated work of reconstructing agreements, confirming delivery, handling exceptions and closing the books. The first customer hypothesis is a small crypto team paying recurring contractors from a Safe. That operator needs one dependable answer: what do we actually owe this vendor now, and what still needs attention?

The product joins the current approved agreement, authorized acceptance, trusted destination, prior payments and remaining obligation. Routine work should move within established authority. Exceptions should reach the right person with the specific evidence and next action. A payment that is pending or uncertain should remain visible until the destination outcome is reconciled.

## Earn trust in stages

Read-only onboarding should be useful before any authority is granted: organize originals, expose missing evidence and make the next review clear. Preparation adds exact obligation amounts and an inspectable plan. Delegation follows only after current policy, bounded on-chain controls, compatible execution, reliable operations and independent recovery are established.

The model helps interpret sources and propose permitted actions. It cannot approve its own evidence, activate terms, change a trusted recipient, enlarge a budget or release funds. When a model is useful, its contribution should be visible against a strong rules baseline: a relevant source association, a well-targeted question or a better admissible suggestion. An explanation by itself is not proof of better judgment.

Current implemented capabilities and externally unverified operations are listed in the [release status](https://www.usecountersign.xyz/docs/release-status). The live software demo is synthetic. Actual customer usage, measured savings, Circle/Safe compatibility, live payment operation and funded protection are not established by passing software tests.

## Prove usefulness before expanding

The next investment is real recurring customer cycles. Measure the customer's normal workflow, strong rules and the same rules plus the agent against identical point-in-time information. Report active human minutes, exact operating cost, false holds, missed confirmed issues, due completion, recovery and repeat use. Separate founder and independent businesses, testnet and mainnet, historical replay and synthetic cases. A read-only pilot can begin without granting payment authority.

Live execution then needs a genuinely compatible Circle path, reviewed deployed controls, explicit allocation, persistent worker custody and an independent recovery exercise. Closing the books needs actual settlement allocations and a bookkeeper's review. Those outcomes, rather than a growing integration list, determine what the product earns permission to do next.

## Make the evidence reusable

Receipts should let a reviewer connect an exact obligation/version and authority to the original operation and destination outcome. Offline verification must state what it cannot establish about current revocation or chain finality. Private documents remain private; public examples require explicit consent and redaction or must be clearly synthetic.

The receipt verifier, local pilot capture/evaluator, recovery fixtures and bounded execution examples are useful building blocks for other systems. Reuse and publication depend on the repository's actual license; this vision does not grant rights or promise a public-source commitment.

## A business that continues operating

A software subscription and separately priced reserved liability remain hypotheses to validate against customer willingness to pay, actual operating cost and exposure. Protection cannot precede accepted limited terms, fully reserved liability and independent adjudication. No revenue, customer count or loss-rate claim is established yet.

Expansion follows demanded workflows. A bookkeeper may need a specific accounting writeback; a contractor may need a clearer acceptance or correction path. General ERP, yield strategies and marketplaces require their own customer evidence and authority boundaries.

Every operating deployment needs an incident owner, reconciliation, support and recovery after a release. Any coverage remains reserved for its contractual duration. The long-term product earns more delegated trust only when customers can see correct outcomes and retain independent control.
